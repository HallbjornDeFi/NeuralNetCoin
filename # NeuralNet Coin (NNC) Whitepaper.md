# NeuralNet Coin (NNC) Whitepaper  
  
## I. Introduction  
  
NeuralNet Coin (NNC) is a decentralized cryptocurrency built on the Solana blockchain, designed to bridge  
human innovation with artificial intelligence. Its mission is to create a reward-driven ecosystem where  
users contribute to AI learning, data generation, and model improvement through a decentralized network.  
  
## II. Vision  
  
NNC envisions a future where human intelligence and artificial intelligence operate symbiotically.  
By decentralizing the development and utilization of AI, NeuralNet Coin promotes transparency, collaboration,  
and fair compensation for human input.  
  
## III. Tokenomics  
  
- Token Name: NeuralNet Coin  
- Symbol: NNC  
- Network: Solana  
- Creator Fee: 3%  
- Use Case: Governance, Data Exchange, AI Model Contribution Rewards  
  
### Creator Fee Rationale  
The 3% creator fee sustains ongoing development, infrastructure maintenance, and community engagement.  
It ensures NeuralNet Coin can grow long-term without hidden inflation or surprise token mints.  
97% of every trade remains with the community, while 3% fuels the builders who maintain and expand the ecosystem.  
  
## IV. Utility  
  
The NNC ecosystem incentivizes participation in AI training and infrastructure building. Users and developers  
can contribute to decentralized machine learning tasks, data annotation, and computational processes, earning  
NNC as compensation. Over time, this creates an open-source AI knowledge network powered by human effort.  
  
## V. Detailed Roadmap  
  
Phase 1: Token Creation & Branding (Q4 2025)  
- Establish NeuralNet Coin identity and community foundation.  
- Launch token via Moonshot and integrate with Solana ecosystem.  
- Begin social media presence and initiate verification process on Solscan and Phantom.  
  
Phase 2: AI Utility Development (Q1–Q2 2026)  
- Launch basic AI infrastructure to support decentralized data and model contributions.  
- Introduce NeuralNet Utility Layer for real-time participation rewards.  
- Begin pilot partnerships with open-source AI developers to test decentralized learning protocols.  
- Deploy data contribution reward smart contracts for transparency.  
  
Phase 3: Neural Network Integration (Q3–Q4 2026)  
- Integrate functional AI nodes where $NNC tokens power network computation.  
- Develop community-driven datasets and allow staking for resource access.  
- Implement NeuralNet API for developers to connect applications and AI agents.  
- Prepare NNC token for cross-platform integration and decentralized data liquidity.  
  
### Phase 4: DAO Governance & Expansion (2027)  
- Establish NeuralNet DAO where holders can vote on system proposals and upgrades.  
- Enable treasury management through NNC-based smart contracts.  
- Expand ecosystem partnerships with AI and blockchain research initiatives.  
- Move toward full decentralization of the NeuralNet AI network.  
  
Phase 5: Long-Term Sustainability (Beyond 2027)  
- Introduce scalable AI model hosting and human-AI collaboration interfaces.  
- Build long-term token stability through deflationary mechanisms and staking rewards.  
- Foster global participation through education, transparency, and open collaboration.  
  
## VI. Governance  
  
The NeuralNet Coin DAO will allow holders to vote on ecosystem proposals, partnerships, and AI integration  
initiatives. Decentralized governance ensures long-term community-driven innovation and transparency.  
  
## VII. Future Outlook  
  
As the demand for decentralized AI grows, NeuralNet Coin positions itself as a central node in the global  
effort to democratize artificial intelligence and data value creation.
