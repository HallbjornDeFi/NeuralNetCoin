# NeuralNet City — Technical Overview (Official Specification)
*A Cyberpunk-Inspired, AI-Driven World for the NeuralNet Coin Ecosystem*

---

## I. Introduction
NeuralNet City is the immersive hub world of the NeuralNet Coin (NNC) ecosystem—an AI-driven, cyberpunk-influenced digital environment that merges decentralized reward systems, phygital commerce, trust-signal detection, and player-owned NFT property.

The world uses clean futurism, grounded industrial realism, and minimal neon accents. It is cyberpunk but never cheesy, cluttered, or dystopian. This is a hopeful cyberpunk environment built around intelligence, contribution, and community.

NeuralNet City is not a metaverse gimmick. It is infrastructure for:
- decentralized social trust,
- phygital economic activity,
- data-driven rewards,
- AI-powered identity,
- and community-guided world expansion.

### **Concept Status**
NeuralNet City is currently a forward-looking concept in the architectural and planning phase.
The full game will move into production only after the NeuralNet Coin ecosystem reaches:
- a larger holder base,
- stronger community engagement,
- and adequate funding to support sustainable development.

This specification defines the long-term intended structure of NeuralNet City, but it should be understood as a conceptual framework until the ecosystem grows enough to support active game development.

---

## II. Vision
NeuralNet City represents a future where humans and AI collaborate inside a functional, decentralized digital world. It unifies:

- the Social-AI trust engine,
- DecentraNet Marketplace,
- NNC phygital commerce,
- NFT-based property,
- and a universal earned currency: Neural Credits (NC).

This world transforms the NNC ecosystem into an interactive, immersive, AI-driven environment.

NeuralNet City uses a hybrid layout for scalability and clarity:

### **Circular Base**

### **Vertical Layers**
- Level 0: Streets

This geometry preserves immersion while supporting world expansion.

## V. Unified Economic Design

### **1. Neural Credits (NC) — Earned Only**
NC is:
- off-chain  
- non-transferable  
- non-purchasable  
- earned through Social-AI trust actions  
- earned from in-city missions  
- redeemable for NNC during epochs  

NC captures and rewards user contribution and accuracy.

### **2. NeuralNet Coin (NNC) — On-Chain**
NNC is used for:
- property ownership  
- phygital goods  
- storefront operations  
- premium world utilities  
- receiving NC conversion redemptions  

NNC is the real-value economic engine.

---

## VI. Hybrid NC → NNC Conversion Model
The conversion system uses:

- fixed base rate  
- dynamic scaling under heavy load  
- weekly or bi-weekly epochs  
- accuracy-weighted NC scoring  
- router fee (2% capped at 25 NNC)  
- anti-exploit controls  

This ensures sustainability and fairness.

## VII. NFT Property System (Hybrid Scarcity)

### **Core Districts: Fixed Supply**

### **Expansion Districts: Controlled Releases**
New supply unlocks via governance votes.

### **Property Utilities (No Pay-to-Win)**
Properties unlock:

- decorative holograms  
- aesthetic light effects  

### **Commercial Plot Revenue**
Storefront owners earn **5% commission** on NNC sales in their plot.

## VIII. Phygital Commerce (Universal Marketplace)
NeuralNet City supports **all product categories**, including:
- kitchenware  
- electronics  
- apparel  
- tools  
- books  
- accessories  
- creator merchandise  
- home goods  
- cyberpunk collectibles  
- tech hardware  

### **Purchase Flow**
1. Items added to cart (bundles supported)  
2. User checks out with **NNC**  
3. Vendor receives NNC  
4. Router fee goes to treasury  
5. Plot owner gets 5% commission  
6. User receives:
   - the real item  
   - an in-game digital replica  

### **Package Tracking**
A holographic tracker displays:
- Received  
- In Transit  
- Out for Delivery  
- Arrived

## IX. Vendor Onboarding — Social-AI Tiered Verification
Vendor access follows the DecentraNet Marketplace model:

### **1. Curated Vendors (Launch)**
Manually selected, safe, reliable.

### **2. Social-AI Verified Vendors (Expansion)**
Automated onboarding via:
- scam/no-scam detection  
- fake engagement filtering  
- trust-score modeling  
- listing integrity checks  
- bot-pattern analysis  

Vendors gain or lose access automatically based on trust signals.

### **3. Fully Open Marketplace (Mature Phase)**
Anyone can list products; Social-AI continuously monitors and auto-moderates fraud.

### **DAO Separation**
DAO **does not approve vendors**.  
Vendor trust is enforced exclusively by the Social-AI engine.
