# NeuralNet Coin (NNC)  
### “Building a Transparent, Ethical Future for Artificial Intelligence”  

---

## 🧠 Overview  
NeuralNet Coin ($NNC) is a decentralized AI-powered cryptocurrency built on the Solana blockchain.  
It bridges human innovation and machine learning through a reward-driven, transparent ecosystem — where verified data, computation, and community participation all contribute to collective intelligence.  

Launched via **Moonshot’s verified SPL contract**, NeuralNet Coin is fully transparent and immutable.  
All token parameters, creator fees, and mint details are publicly verifiable on Solscan, ensuring compliance with industry standards and audit integrity.  

---

## 🔍 Mission  
To create a **decentralized AI economy** where people, devices, and AI systems cooperate fairly through transparent value exchange.  
Every contribution — whether data, code, or conversation — earns measurable rewards in $NNC, powering an open and ethical AI infrastructure.

## 🧩 Core Vision  
NeuralNet Coin envisions a transparent and self-sustaining **Social-AI Economy** — where blockchain validates every contribution to machine learning, ensuring human input is respected, measured, and rewarded.  

Key principles include:  
- **Transparency:** All token movements and governance votes are on-chain.  
- **Security:** Smart contracts undergo CertiK review and open-source publication.  
- **Ethical AI:** Rewarding human creativity and verified data over algorithmic exploitation.  
- **Community Governance:** Decisions driven by DAO proposals with 5,000 NNC minimum stake.

## 🛡️ Security & Compliance Philosophy  
Security is prioritized above expansion.  
Before any exchange listing or ecosystem integration, NeuralNet Coin undergoes:  
1. **Independent audit verification** (CertiK).  
2. **Public reporting** of audit results and on-chain metrics.  
3. **DAO approval** for major governance or emission changes.  

This ensures the ecosystem evolves transparently and lawfully under U.S. SEC and EU MiCA digital-asset frameworks.
