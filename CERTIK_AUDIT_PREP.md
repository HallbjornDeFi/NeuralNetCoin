## Audit Objectives
CertiK will assess the following critical areas of NeuralNet Coin’s ecosystem and token architecture:

### 1. Smart Contract Integrity
- Validation of mint and freeze authority configuration.  
- Confirmation that liquidity, supply, and fee logic match the whitepaper and tokenomics.  
- Ensure all fee, staking, and emission functions operate securely and transparently.

### 2. Security & Compliance
- Verify resistance to common Solana-based vulnerabilities (e.g., reentrancy, overflow, privilege escalation).  
- Confirm compliance with international crypto-asset security and KYC standards.  
- Validate that ownership and supply controls are decentralized and tamper-resistant.

### 3. DAO Governance
- Confirm DAO staking threshold of 5,000 NNC for proposal submission.  
- Evaluate voting weight distribution and whale-prevention mechanisms.  
- Verify treasury transparency and proposal execution logic.

### 4. Ecosystem Functionality
- Review reward and emission mechanics per the deflationary schedule.  
- Validate vesting structure and staking pool release schedule.  
- Assess system readiness for CertiK Skynet live monitoring post-verification.
